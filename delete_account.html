Account Deletion

Delete your account

You can request deletion of your Master Chess Pro account and associated account data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email us from the email address associated with your Master Chess Pro account, if applicable.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete Master Chess Pro Account”.
  3. Include your in-app username or account identifier if you have one. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.
  4. We will verify the request when necessary and process eligible deletion requests as soon as reasonably possible.
manucerhasanov10@gmail.com

What data may be deleted

Depending on the account and features used, deletion may include:

  • Account profile information and account identifiers stored by Master Chess Pro.
  • Cloud-saved game progress, achievements and app preferences linked to the account.
  • Backend records associated with the account where deletion is technically and legally permitted.

What may be retained

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute-resolution or platform-compliance purposes.

  • Google Play purchase records may remain available through Google Play and related billing systems.
  • Transaction and entitlement records handled by RevenueCat may be retained according to applicable legal and service requirements.
  • Backups may persist for a limited period before being overwritten or deleted.
Deleting a Master Chess Pro account does not itself cancel an active Google Play subscription. Subscription cancellation must be managed through Google Play.
